FAQ:

Q: What is an SSD?
A: An SSD (Solid State Drive) is a high-speed storage device that uses flash memory instead of spinning disks, providing faster performance and better reliability.

Q: What are enterprise SSDs?
A: Enterprise SSDs are storage devices designed for servers, data centers, AI workloads, and mission-critical systems requiring high reliability and performance.

Q: What is edge AI?
A: Edge AI refers to running artificial intelligence models directly on local or embedded devices instead of relying completely on cloud servers.

Q: What are embedded systems?
A: Embedded systems are specialized computing systems designed for dedicated applications inside larger hardware or industrial systems.

Q: Why is low-power computing important?
A: Low-power systems improve efficiency, thermal performance, energy usage, and battery life in embedded and industrial devices.
