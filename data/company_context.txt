The global semiconductor landscape is currently undergoing a seismic shift, characterized by a 
transition from centralized global supply chains to localized, resilient ecosystems driven by the 
twin imperatives of national security and technological self-reliance. In this context, the 
emergence of MiPhi Semiconductors Private Limited represents a seminal moment for the 
Indian electronics industry. Established as a high-stakes joint venture between Micromax 
Informatics, an Indian consumer electronics titan, and Phison Electronics Corporation, a global 
leader in NAND flash controllers, MiPhi is not merely a manufacturer but a strategic design-led 
entity aimed at capturing the high-value segments of the data storage and artificial intelligence 
(AI) infrastructure markets. By marrying Phison’s twenty-four years of engineering 
pedigree—encompassing over 2,100 patents and an extensive library of controller IP—with 
Micromax’s manufacturing legacy and deep understanding of the Indian market, MiPhi is 
positioned to redefine the "Make in India" initiative from simple assembly to complex, indigenous 
semiconductor design. This report provides an exhaustive analysis of MiPhi’s corporate 
structure, product portfolio, technical innovations in AI infrastructure, and its strategic role in 
fostering a sovereign digital ecosystem in India.

The "Design-First" Strategic Imperative and National 
Sovereignty 
The strategic underpinning of MiPhi Semiconductors is its uncompromising "design-first" 
philosophy. Historically, the Indian semiconductor landscape has been dominated by Electronic 
Manufacturing Services (EMS) companies that focus on assembly, testing, marking, and 
packaging (ATMP) or Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test (OSAT). MiPhi breaks this 
mold by focusing on the higher-value segments of the semiconductor stack: firmware 
architecture, controller design, and ASIC optimization. This approach is not merely a business 
choice but a necessity for building "digital sovereignty," as it ensures that the critical controllers 
and firmware—the "brains" of the storage device—are designed and controlled within the 
country. 
By licensing Phison's core technologies and expanding upon them locally, MiPhi is creating a 
uniquely Indian intellectual property portfolio. This includes the development of localized 
firmware that can be customized for specific Indian requirements, such as enhanced data 
security for government sectors or power-optimized performance for data centers operating in 
varied environmental conditions. This strategy directly supports the Government of India’s 
Production Linked Incentive (PLI) schemes, particularly the ₹30,000 crore Component PLI 
announced in March 2025, which encourages the domestic manufacture of high-value IT 
components. MiPhi’s ability to move from simple assembly to SMT (Surface Mount Technology), 
then to components, and finally to full design, represents the complete evolutionary trajectory 



intended for the Indian technology sector. 

Enterprise Storage Ecosystem: Performance, 
Capacity, and Mission-Critical Reliability 
MiPhi has positioned itself as the first homegrown brand to design and manufacture 
enterprise-grade Solid State Drives (SSDs) in India. The company’s enterprise portfolio is 
meticulously categorized to address the diverse needs of modern data centers, ranging from 
high-performance computing (HPC) and AI training to read-intensive archival storage and 
mission-critical boot drives. 

Performance X-Series: The Frontier of Data Throughput 
The X-Series represents MiPhi's high-end enterprise solution, utilizing the latest PCIe Gen5 and 
Gen4 interfaces to deliver deterministic performance for data-intensive workloads. 

●​ X200 Technical Analysis: The X200 is a flagship PCIe Gen5x4 dual-port SSD, designed 
to support the increasing throughput requirements of modern servers. It offers sequential 
read speeds of up to 14.8 GB/s and random read performance of 3,000 KIOPs. Available 
in form factors such as U.2 (2.5") and E3.S, it scales from 1.6 TB up to 30.72 TB. The 
series is differentiated by its endurance ratings: the X200P (1 Drive Write Per Day - 
DWPD) for read-optimized tasks and the X200E (3 DWPD) for mixed-use workloads. Its 
active power consumption of 25W is optimized for high-density server deployments where 
thermal management is a critical constraint. 

●​ X100 Performance Profile: Operating on a PCIe Gen4x4 platform and utilizing MiPhi's 
patented CPU architecture, the X100 provides up to 7.4 GB/s sequential read speeds and 
1,750 KIOPs random read performance. It supports capacities up to 30.72 TB and 
features enhanced security and compliance, including FIPS-140 standards, making it an 
ideal choice for high-security enterprise and government environments. 

Data Center D-Series: Scaling the Zettabyte Economy 
As global data generation is projected to exceed 180 zettabytes by 2025, data centers require 
storage solutions that prioritize density and power efficiency. The D-Series addresses this by 
offering extreme capacity and optimized Quality of Service (QoS). 

●​ D200V High-Capacity SSD: The D200V is a revolutionary read-intensive PCIe Gen5 
SSD that achieves an unprecedented capacity of up to 122.88 TB per drive. A single 
D200V unit can store the equivalent data of ten traditional HDDs, enabling massive 
reductions in data center rack space and power consumption. It delivers 14 GB/s 
sequential read speeds and 3,000K IOPS random read performance, utilizing high-density 
3D NAND technology to drive the trend of efficient data storage. 

●​ D100 Versatility: The D100 is designed for consistency across intensive read and write 
commands, providing excellent QoS. It utilizes a PCIe Gen4x4 dual-port interface and is 
available in M.2 2280, 22110, and E1.S form factors. Capacities range from 480 GB to 
3.84 TB, with the E1.S version reaching sequential read speeds of 6.8 GB/s.

AI Infrastructure Innovation: The aiDAPTIV+ 
Framework and Model Slicing 
One of MiPhi’s most transformative contributions to the technology sector is the aiDAPTIV+ 
suite, a turnkey hybrid software and hardware solution designed to democratize AI training and 
inference. As Large Language Models (LLMs) like Llama 3.1 scale to hundreds of billions of 
parameters, the industry has encountered a "memory wall," where the cost of AI infrastructure is 
increasingly driven by the need for massive amounts of expensive High Bandwidth Memory 
(HBM) on GPUs. 

The Mechanism of aiDAPTIV+ 
The aiDAPTIV+ solution addresses the "memory wall" by decoupling compute from memory. 
Traditional AI architectures tightly couple GPU accelerators with model weights and activations 
stored in HBM. When the model size exceeds the HBM capacity, organizations are forced to 
purchase higher-end GPUs or scale GPU counts linearly, leading to exorbitant costs and 
frequent out-of-memory (OOM) failures. 



The aiDAPTIV+ framework utilizes specialized MiPhi NVMe SSDs (aiDAPTIVCache) as an 
active memory tier. The middleware operates through several sophisticated mechanisms: 

1.​ Model Slicing: The LLM is sliced into smaller segments that can fit within the available 
GPU memory. 

2.​ Dynamic Streaming: Model weights and activations are streamed dynamically from the 
NVMe storage to the GPU. Pending slices are swapped with finished ones, allowing the 
GPU to focus on tensor operations while the NVMe storage handles the memory capacity. 

3.​ High-Endurance SLC NAND: Because this process involves continuous data swapping, 
the aiDAPTIV+ SSDs utilize SLC NAND with advanced error correction, delivering an 
industry-leading endurance of 100 DWPD and a 5-year warranty. 

This approach allows organizations to train and fine-tune massive models—including Llama-2 
70B and Llama 3.1 405B—on commodity workstation hardware or a single AI PC, reducing the 
cost of GPU infrastructure by as much as 90%. 

Strategic Collaboration with Intel and SUSE: GPU-Less AI Training 
In September 2025, MiPhi announced a breakthrough collaboration with Intel and SUSE that 
further disrupts the AI infrastructure market. This collaboration has resulted in a solution that 
eliminates the need for GPUs entirely for training small-scale AI models. By optimizing Intel 
CPU architectures—specifically those featuring Advanced Matrix Extensions (AMX)—and 
utilizing SUSE’s enterprise-grade Linux platform, MiPhi has enabled AI training to occur directly 
on CPUs with dramatically improved efficiency. 
This "cross-industry synergy" is particularly relevant for startups and developers in emerging 
markets who face hardware limitations. By repurposing CPUs for tasks once dominated by 
GPUs, MiPhi is significantly lowering the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) for AI workloads, 
including NLP, computer vision, and recommendation systems.

Embedded Systems and Automotive Solutions: 
Durability for Harsh Environments 
The embedded semiconductor market requires products that can withstand extreme 
temperatures and vibration while maintaining high data integrity. MiPhi addresses this through 
its specialized eMMC, UFS, and BGA SSD solutions. 

●​ eMMC Solutions: MiPhi's eMMC lineup features automotive and industrial-grade 
durability, empowering next-generation devices with remarkable speeds. The PS8231 and 
PS8232 controllers support capacities from 32GB to 512GB, offering sequential read 
speeds up to 320 MB/s and operating within a temperature range of -25°C to 85°C 
(Tcase). These specifications make them suitable for telematics, industrial controllers, and 
ruggedized computing. 

●​ UFS (Universal Flash Storage): Designed to supercharge next-generation mobile 
devices, UFS technology fuels unrivaled responsiveness for every tap and swipe. It is 
critical for the evolving mobile ecosystem where AI-driven features demand high-speed 
local storage. 

●​ BGA SSD (Phison MPT560): For space-constrained applications, the MPT560 BGA SSD 
offers PCIe Gen 4x4 speeds in a compact 1620 package. With capacities ranging from 
64GB to 1024GB and performance reaching 3650 MB/s read and 2900 MB/s write, it 
brings workstation-level storage to the most compact devices.

Macroeconomic Context: India vs. Vietnam and the 
China+1 Strategy

companies are seeking to diversify their supply chains away from China—a trend known as the 



"China+1" strategy. Co-founder Rahul Sharma has argued that India is a stronger long-term

more sustainable partner for Western technology giants looking for long-term stability and a 
domestic market that can absorb locally manufactured high-tech products.
