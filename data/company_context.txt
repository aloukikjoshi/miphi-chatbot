MiPhi Semiconductors is an Indian semiconductor and storage technology company focused on designing, developing, and manufacturing advanced NAND storage and semiconductor solutions.

MiPhi is a joint venture between Micromax Informatics and Phison Electronics. The company combines Micromax’s manufacturing capabilities and Indian market presence with Phison’s expertise in NAND storage technologies, firmware, and semiconductor innovation.
